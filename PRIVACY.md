# Privacy Policy — Secret Chronicles (Android)

Last updated: 25 August 2026

## Short version

This game collects nothing, sends nothing, and needs no network connection.

## What the app stores

Everything the game writes stays on your device, in the app's own private
storage, and is removed when you uninstall the app:

- your savegames and level progress;
- your settings (audio volume, controls);
- screenshots you take from inside the game.

## What the app does not do

- No analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising SDK.
- No account, no login, no personal data of any kind.
- No network permission is requested, so the app cannot transmit anything.
- No data is shared with anyone, because none is collected.

## Children

The game contains no ads, no purchases and no communication features. Nothing
is collected from any user, whatever their age.
